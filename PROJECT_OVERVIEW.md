# Atelier Studio: The Future of Creative Design

**Atelier Studio** is a premium, AI-powered design workspace built for professional creators, entrepreneurs, and designers who want to craft high-end brand identities with ease and precision.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. AI-Driven Design Studio
*   **Neural Creative Engine**: Generate logo inspirations and brand assets instantly using advanced AI models.
*   **Professional Editor**: A full-featured workspace to refine text, typography, colors, and layouts.
*   **Precision Tools**: High-end controls for pixel-perfect adjustments.

### 2. Modern & Premium UI
*   **Luminous Dark Theme**: A sophisticated indigo-purple aesthetic designed for maximum focus and visual appeal.
*   **Curved Navigation**: A fluid, animated bottom navigation bar for a seamless user experience.
*   **Responsive Design**: Optimized for all modern mobile devices using `flutter_screenutil`.

### 3. Secure Authentication
*   **One-Tap Login**: Fast and secure "Continue with Google" integration via Firebase.
*   **Identity Sync**: Automatically fetches your Google profile (Name, Email, Photo) to personalize your experience.

### 4. Smart Project Management
*   **Your Atelier**: A dedicated space to manage, view, and edit your saved designs.
*   **History & Templates**: Quickly access previous work or start from professionally curated templates.

### 5. Advanced Profile Controls
*   **Notification Center**: Stay updated with the latest AI models and promotional offers.
*   **Privacy First**: Securely manage your data with options to sign out or permanently delete your account.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
*   **Framework**: Flutter (Dart)
*   **State Management**: GetX (MVVM Architecture)
*   **Backend**: Firebase (Auth & Core)
*   **Styling**: Google Fonts (Outfit, Playfair Display, Dancing Script)
*   **Animations**: Curved Navigation Bar & Animated Containers
